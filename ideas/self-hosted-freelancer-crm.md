# Self-Hosted Freelancer CRM

## What
Open source CRM specifically designed for freelancers — simple client tracking, project timelines, time tracking, and invoicing.

## Why
SuiteCRM and Dolibarr are too enterprise and complex. Freelancers don't need 500 features — they need simple client management and getting paid on time.

## Market Gap
- SuiteCRM: Too complex, enterprise-focused, ugly UI
- Dolibarr: French interface by default, overwhelming for freelancers
- HubSpot: Free tier exists but bakes in SaaS dependency
- **Gap**: Simple, beautiful CRM that freelancers actually want to use

## Tech Stack
- **Option A**: PHP (Laravel) - natural fit for self-hosted
- **Option B**: Node.js - modern stack, easy deployment
- **Database**: SQLite or MySQL
- **Frontend**: Simple React or Blade templates
- **Invoice**: PDF generation with wkhtmltopdf

## What's Close
- [Feng Office](https://fengoffice.com) - Old UI, not freelancer-focused
- [SuiteCRM](https://suitecrm.com) - Too enterprise
- [Dolibarr](https://www.dolibarr.org) - Too complex, dated
- [Capsule](https://capsulecrm.com) - Good but SaaS-only
- [Streak](https://www.streak.com) - CRM in Gmail, not standalone

## Revenue Model
- Self-host for individual freelancers (free)
- Premium support and hosting ($29-99/month)
- Marketplace for templates and integrations
