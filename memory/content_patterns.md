# Content Patterns — What Makes Lists Succeed

## Structure Winners
- **Hook in first 50 words** — don't bury the lead
- **TL;DR box after intro** — for scannable readers
- **Item length consistency** — avoid one item being 300 words while next is 30
- **Each item answers "why"** — why did this make the list?
- **Specific over generic** — "iPhone 15 Pro's A17 chip" beats "iPhone 15"

## Engagement Triggers
- Numbered lists with satisfying count (10, 15, 20, 25)
- Photos/images per item (if platform supports)
- "Surprising fact" in each item
- Clear hierarchy (best to worst OR categorized)

## SEO Wins
- Primary keyword in H1 + first 100 words
- External links to authoritative sources (gov, edu, major publications)
- Natural secondary keyword mentions throughout
- FAQ section targeting long-tail questions
- Internal links structure in place

## Failures to Avoid
- Thin items (under 50 words each)
- No unique angle in title
- Sources that look spammy or unreliable
- Keyword stuffing or forced placement
- Listicle format with no substance
