# Keyword Patterns — What Makes Topics Win

## High-Performing Topic Characteristics

### Angle Types That Win
- **Situational:** "Best X for [specific situation]" (e.g., "Best Laptops for Gaming in 2026")
- **Comparative:** "X vs Y" or "Best X Alternatives"
- **Budget:** "Best Free X" or "Best X Under $50"
- **Beginner:** "X for Beginners" or "Complete Guide to X"
- **Emerging:** "X in 2026" or "Next Big Thing in X"

### Anti-Patterns (Low Performers)
- Generic "Top 10 X" with no angle
- Outdated topics with no 2025/2026 updates
- Overly broad topics with thin coverage
- Niche too small for meaningful traffic
